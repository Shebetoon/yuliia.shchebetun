Act II · View

Any brief, one standard of quality.

I've been working in AI photo and video generation for a year and a half — I came straight into this field with no background in traditional photography or videography. That gave me a different way of thinking about production: I build the shoot around the prompt from scratch, instead of adapting a camera to AI.

I work with brands and agencies — from ad films to product photography — and I don't stick to one niche: I take on any brief and pick the right photo and video models for it. Alongside commercial projects, I make personal work: short films, music videos, animation.
